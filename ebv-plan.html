Your diet is already strong. These targeted swaps address the specific foods working against your EBV and inflammation.

✅ WHAT YOU'RE DOING RIGHT
● Grass-fed beef — zero oxalate, high lysine, anti-inflammatory
● Free-range eggs — zero oxalate, high lysine, nutrient powerhouse
● Butter — anti-inflammatory, Bulletproof staple
● MCT oil — brain fuel, anti-inflammatory
● Avocados — low oxalate, healthy fat
● Blueberries — low oxalate, high antioxidant
● Sauerkraut — fermented, great for gut
● Coffee + MCT — low oxalate, Asprey approved
● Gluten-free — reduces inflammation, especially with EBV
🔄 CRITICAL SWAPS
1. Peanut Butter → Macadamia or Coconut Butter
WHY: Peanuts are high oxalate AND very high arginine — the amino acid EBV needs to replicate. This is your #1 dietary antagonist.
✓ Macadamia butter — lowest oxalate nut, low arginine, tastes great
✓ Coconut butter (manna) — lauric acid converts to monolaurin
2. Almond Milk → Coconut or Macadamia Milk
WHY: Almonds are extremely high oxalate. Liquid form concentrates it.
✓ Unsweetened coconut milk — low oxalate, contains lauric acid
✓ Macadamia milk — great but pricier
3. Mixed Nuts & Cashews → Macadamia Nuts
WHY: Most mixes contain almonds/peanuts. Cashews are moderate-high oxalate.
✓ Macadamias — the one nut you can snack freely on
✓ Coconut chips — satisfying crunch, low oxalate
4. Sweet Potatoes — Change Prep & Reduce
WHY: 70-90mg oxalate per half cup baked.
✓ BOIL instead of bake (leaches oxalate into water)
✓ Limit to ½ cup, 2-3x per week max
✓ Better: peeled new potatoes (red-skin), boiled — half the oxalate
5. Snack Audit
Catalina Crunch — check for almond flour. Boulder Canyon — check oil type. Siete — cassava ok, avoid almond flour ones.
✓ Better: macadamias, coconut chips, jerky, pork rinds, olives, hard-boiled eggs, rice crackers + coconut butter
🚫 HIGH-ARGININE FOODS — FEEDS EBV
✕ Peanuts/peanut butter — #1 priority to remove
✕ Almonds/almond milk — high oxalate + arginine
✕ Cashews — moderate-high both
✕ Chocolate/cacao — high oxalate + arginine
✕ Oats — moderate oxalate + arginine
✕ Sesame seeds — high oxalate
✕ Soy products — inflammatory + arginine
⚠️ MODERATE — KEEP BUT WATCH
~ Strawberries — moderate oxalate. Shift ratio toward more blueberries.
~ Bananas — OK but high sugar. Keep to 1/day.
~ Honey — better than sugar but still sugar. Use sparingly.
⏳ IMPORTANT: TRANSITION GRADUALLY

Make these swaps over 2-3 weeks, not overnight. Cutting oxalates too fast can trigger "oxalate dumping" — where stored crystals release and cause flareups. Your pattern of cycling between food types may have been causing this already. Consistency is key.

Understanding the inflammation cascade and where each supplement intervenes.

🔬 THE NLRP3 CASCADE
Cell Stress → NLRP3 activates → IL-1β released → IL-6 triggered → Liver → hsCRP
🧠 BRAIN LOOP
Amyloid → triggers NLRP3 → creates more amyloid → self-reinforcing loop
🦠 EBV CONNECTION

EBV has a protein (BILF1) that evades the NLRP3 inflammasome. When NLRP3 is properly activated, it can actually impair viral replication. The supplements target multiple points in this cascade.
